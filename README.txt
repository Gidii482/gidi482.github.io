ONLINE JOBS AFRICA - STARTER WEBSITE

1. Open the files and replace the placeholder site details.
2. Upload the complete folder to a GitHub repository.
3. Enable GitHub Pages in Settings > Pages.
4. Replace YOUR-USERNAME and YOUR-REPOSITORY in robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
5. Create an Adsterra publisher account and add your site.
6. Replace the text "Adsterra placement" with the actual Adsterra code supplied for your chosen ad format.
7. Before launch, customize the Contact and Privacy pages.
8. Add your own original articles and verify all job/platform information.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not click your own ads.
- Do not use bots, fake traffic, click exchanges or misleading redirects.
- Follow Adsterra, GitHub, Google and applicable advertising/privacy policies.
- Advertising revenue is not guaranteed.

The site is intentionally lightweight and uses no external frameworks.
