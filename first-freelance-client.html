How to Get Your First Freelance Client

Winning your first client is easier when you focus on one clear service and demonstrate what you can deliver.

ADVERTISEMENT

Adsterra placement

Practical steps

  • Choose one service rather than advertising everything
  • Create two or three realistic portfolio samples
  • Write short proposals that address the client's exact need
  • Start with achievable projects and collect honest reviews
  • Improve your profile after every application

Do your own research

Availability, eligibility, payment methods and job conditions can change. Always verify current information directly with the platform or employer before applying.

Tip: Focus on legitimate skills and real clients. Avoid fake traffic, fake reviews, automated applications that violate platform rules and any scheme promising guaranteed income.
